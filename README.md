# CandiCore

**Turn career goals into a clearer path forward.**

CandiCore is a career-readiness workspace built to help candidates understand where they stand, strengthen how they present themselves, and approach interviews with greater clarity. From evaluating a resume to organizing professional experience, the platform brings key aspects of career preparation into one evolving application.

## Overview

Career growth is rarely a straight line. Knowing which skills to improve, how to communicate your experience, and where to focus your preparation can make the process more manageable.

CandiCore is being developed around this challenge, combining resume intelligence, guided resume creation, and a foundation for personalized preparation. Its goal is to turn scattered career-building activities into a more structured and actionable experience.

## Features

### 📊 Resume Intelligence
- Upload PDF resumes for automated analysis.
- Assess resume quality through a scoring interface.
- Surface strengths, potential weaknesses, and missing skills.
- Explore role suggestions and targeted improvement recommendations.

### 📝 Guided Resume Creation
- Build a resume through a structured, multi-step workflow.
- Organize personal details, professional summaries, technical skills, work experience, projects, and education.
- Keep career information organized and easier to refine.

### 📈 Career Readiness Dashboard
- A centralized interface for career preparation activities.
- A foundation for bringing progress indicators, performance insights, and preparation milestones together.

### 🔐 Application Experience
- Login interface and current-user integration.
- Reusable UI components and organized application architecture.
- Responsive layouts designed for different screen sizes.

## What's Next?

CandiCore is intended to evolve beyond resume evaluation into a more comprehensive career-development workspace.

### 🎯 Focused Interview Practice
Structured practice experiences for technical interviews, behavioral questions, and coding challenges.

### 🧭 Skill-Gap Mapping
Help candidates connect their current skill sets with the competencies expected for their target roles.

### 🧠 Adaptive Preparation Paths
Personalized learning recommendations that help candidates decide what to study and practise next.

### 📋 Actionable Performance Insights
Clear feedback on areas of strength and opportunities for improvement, supporting a more deliberate preparation process.

### 📄 Enhanced Resume Management
Improved resume editing, previewing, persistence, and export capabilities.

### 📊 Progress and Readiness Tracking
A more complete view of preparation milestones and development over time.

## Vision

CandiCore aims to make career preparation less fragmented and more intentional.

By connecting resume quality, skill development, and interview readiness, the platform aspires to help candidates make better-informed decisions about their next steps and approach professional opportunities with a stronger sense of direction.
